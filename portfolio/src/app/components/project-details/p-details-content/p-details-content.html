Overview

The current systems for managing fuel stations often rely heavily on manual processes or fragmented digital tools that lack integration. Log On the Go was conceptualized to provide a unified platform that brings modern software practices to this traditional sector.

02

Problem

Fuel station managers face challenges with reconciliation of sales, tracking inventory in real-time, and managing shift handovers securely. Discrepancies often lead to financial losses and compromised operational integrity.

03

Solution

A robust web application providing a centralized dashboard for station operations. By digitizing logs and enforcing a strict maker-checker workflow for crucial data entries, the system ensures data validity before it affects inventory or financial reports.
